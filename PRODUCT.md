# ShliachFlow — Product Brief

Short source of truth for what the site is for. Detailed history lives in `docs/`. Update this file when the positioning changes.

## Who it's for
Chabad shluchim and Jewish nonprofit leaders who are already stretched (shiurim, fundraising, Yomim Tovim) and want professional digital work done without managing it or explaining their world from scratch.

## What we want to be known for
"The website and AI guy for shluchim." Run by Rabbi Yisroel Chaim Shuchat, a fellow shliach in S. Pedro, CA.

## Service hierarchy (confirmed Oct 2026)
1. **Websites** (flagship): new sites, refreshes, mini sites, camp sites, anything website-related. Custom-coded, or on a platform (Squarespace, ChabadOne, ChabadSuite).
2. **AI & Technology Consulting** (core): where AI and the right tools actually save time, then getting them set up.
3. **DAAS CRM** (core): daascrm.app. Developed by a fellow shliach, offered and supported by ShliachFlow (customer-facing side: marketing, support). Sell it on: not complex or overwhelming, AI does data entry and donor research, tells you who to reach out to, keeps relationships and giving together. Keep it short and link out; no pricing on this site.
4. **Coming soon:** AI Agents; a website platform for shluchim (works alone or with DAAS).
5. **Also available (small):** books and learning materials (writing, editing, translating; AI-assisted, personally reviewed).

Dropped: tech troubleshooting, standalone graphics/flyers, CRM data cleanup, the old Digital Marketing add-on.
