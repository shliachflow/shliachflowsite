About

Yisroel Chaim Shuchat

I'm a Chabad shliach in S. Pedro, CA.

I've been figuring out tech for my own Chabad House since I got here — websites, donor systems, email, the whole backend. A lot of it I had to work out on my own. When AI tools started getting serious, I learned how to use them properly, and it changed what I'm able to deliver.

At some point I started helping other shluchim — first in WhatsApp groups, then with bigger projects. The need was obvious: someone who already understood the world and could handle the digital side without needing a briefing. That's ShliachFlow.
